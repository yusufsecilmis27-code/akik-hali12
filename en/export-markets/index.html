Our export markets

Wholesale carpets from Gaziantep to the world: Turkic states and CIS, Arab countries, Europe and North America.

As Akik Halı (Emin Seçilmiş Carpet), we export wholesale carpets in container loads to carpet importers, wholesalers and retail chains. Choose your country or city and we will send you a wholesale price list on WhatsApp. We prepare all export documents; delivery terms can be EXW, FOB (Mersin / Istanbul) or CIF.
