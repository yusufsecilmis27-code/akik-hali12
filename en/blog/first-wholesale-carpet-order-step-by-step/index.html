Your first wholesale carpet order, step by step: from quote to delivery

27 November 2026

Starting with a new carpet supplier can feel risky. With us, the process takes a few short steps, and at every step you are in direct contact with the owner of the company.

1. Ask for the price list

Write to us on WhatsApp with the products you are interested in and your country or city. We send you our current wholesale price list and product photos. Every product on our website has its own page; just send us its code.

2. Choose products, sizes and quantities

Our stock rugs ship right away. Our series qualities are produced in the size and weight you need. For export, the minimum is one 20' container, and designs and sizes can be mixed.

3. Proforma invoice and container plan

We send you a proforma invoice and a container plan. You choose the delivery terms: EXW, FOB (Mersin / Istanbul) or CIF.

4. Advance payment and production

T/T 30% advance starts the order; L/C is negotiable. Stock items are ready in 7–10 days and production orders in 25–35 days.

5. Photos, balance and loading

Before loading, you receive photos and videos of your goods. The 70% balance is paid before loading.

6. Documents and tracking

We send the commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and, depending on your country, EUR.1, A.TR or Form A, and follow your shipment until it arrives.

Ordering within Türkiye is even simpler: minimum one bale, payment by bank transfer or credit card, invoiced sales and delivery to all 81 provinces.

Do you have a question? Write to us and we will reply quickly.
