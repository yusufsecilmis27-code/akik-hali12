Non-slip rugs and floor coverings wholesale: a buying guide for retailers

27 November 2026

Non-slip rugs are among the most requested products in flooring and home textile stores, because they are safe to use in kitchens, hallways, bathrooms and children's rooms. When buying wholesale, a few points help you choose the right product.

Digital print non-slip rugs

Digital printing allows a very wide choice of designs and colours. We produce these rugs in any size you need, in the weight and quality you choose. Pick modern, classic or kids' designs according to your customers.

Non-slip roll carpets

Roll carpets are ideal for hallways, stairs and custom-sized areas. Our non-slip roll carpet comes in widths from 80 cm to 300 cm, with weights of 950, 1,100, 1,200 and 1,500 g/m². Retailers can cut and sell it by the metre.

Why weight matters

Weight per square metre defines how dense and durable a rug is. Lighter qualities suit the budget segment, heavier ones suit customers who want long life. Because we can produce the same design in different weights, one design can cover several price points.

Stock or production?

Stock items are ready in 7–10 days and production orders in 25–35 days. A good approach is to start with stock designs, then produce the best sellers in your own sizes.

How to order

For export, the minimum is one 20' container and different products can be mixed. We prepare all export documents and can produce under your own brand. Write to us on WhatsApp for photos and wholesale prices of our non-slip rugs.
